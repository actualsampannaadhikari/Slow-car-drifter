Slow Drifter

An endless, quiet road. No score, no timer, no finish line. Pick a speed and let the hills roll by.

SteerA and D, or the arrow keys. On touch, hold the left or right side of the screen.
SpeedW or Up to speed up, S or Down to slow down. The car holds its speed.
AutopilotPress Space and just watch the scenery.
BoostHold Shift to burn nitro. Grab the gold orbs on the road to refill it.
TrafficCars drive both ways. Overtake and near-miss for cash, and avoid crashes.
LevelsReach the distance goal to get paid, then spend it in the garage (G) on faster cars.
MoreR weather, T time of day, C camera, V next car, M sound, H hide the display, P menu.
